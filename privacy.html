Privacy Policy

This policy applies to Vision API 20260726-01, a private business integration used by authorized administrators to manage Meta business assets and advertising operations.

Effective date: August 1, 2026

Information we process

The application may process Meta account and business identifiers; Page and advertising-account metadata; catalog, data set, pixel, campaign, ad set, and ad metadata; permission status; delivery status; and operational diagnostics required to perform administrator-requested actions. Access tokens and application credentials are used only to authenticate authorized API requests.

How we use information

Information is used only to provide requested business-management, catalog, measurement, advertising, reporting, and support functions. The application does not sell personal data and does not use data for unrelated profiling.

Data sharing

Information is shared only with Meta APIs, service providers strictly necessary to perform the requested operation, and authorized business administrators.

Data retention and security

Credentials are stored using operating-system-protected credential storage. Operational records are retained only as long as needed for the service, security, audit, or legal obligations.

Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time from Facebook Settings under Business Integrations or from the relevant Meta Business settings. Revocation stops future API access by the application.

Contact

Privacy and deletion questions should be sent to the authorized business administrator through the same verified channel used to provide access to the application.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the application's processing changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
