Data Deletion

Follow these instructions to revoke access and request deletion of application-held information associated with an authorized HT615 Measurement Hub connection.

Effective date: August 18, 2026

Request deletion

  1. 1
    Revoke application access

    Open Facebook Settings, go to Business Integrations, and remove HT615 Measurement Hub from the relevant connection.

  2. 2
    Contact the authorized administrator

    Use the same verified business channel through which access was granted.

  3. 3
    Identify the connection

    State which Meta business connection the deletion request concerns. Do not send credentials or raw identifiers.

What happens next

After authority is verified, application-held operational information associated with that connection will be deleted or irreversibly anonymized within 30 days, except records that must be retained for security, fraud prevention, audit or legal obligations.

Request status

The authorized administrator provides status through the same verified business channel used for the request. This public page does not expose private deletion records.

Security reminder

Never include a password, access token, application secret, cookie or authorization code in a deletion request.
