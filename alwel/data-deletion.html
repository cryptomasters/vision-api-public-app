Your information and access

Data deletion

Request deletion of information held by Ne Cong Alwel Tisinyuk for an authorized business connection.

  1. Revoke further access

    In the applicable Meta Business Settings or Business Integrations settings, remove this application's authorization or ask the authorized administrator to revoke the relevant technical-user access. This stops future access; it does not by itself erase historical records.

  2. Contact the administrator

    Use the same verified business channel through which access was granted. State the application name, the business connection concerned and the information you want deleted. Do not provide passwords, tokens, cookies or application secrets.

  3. Receive confirmation

    After verifying your authority, the administrator reviews the request and deletes or anonymizes application-held information that no longer needs to be retained. Records required for security, dispute resolution or legal obligations may be retained as permitted by law. The administrator communicates the outcome through the same verified channel.

Records held by other services

A request to this application concerns its own records. Data maintained independently by Meta or another provider is subject to that provider's settings and deletion process. Campaigns and other business assets are not automatically deleted by a privacy request.
