Data Deletion

Follow these instructions to revoke access and request deletion of application-held operational information associated with your business connection.

Effective date: August 1, 2026

Request deletion

  1. 1
    Revoke application access

    Open Facebook Settings, go to Business Integrations, and remove Vision API from the relevant business connection.

  2. 2
    Contact the authorized administrator

    Use the same verified business channel through which access to the application was granted.

  3. 3
    Identify the connection

    State which Meta business connection the deletion request concerns. Do not send access tokens, passwords, or other credentials.

Request checklist ready

Revoke access, contact the authorized administrator through the verified channel, and identify the relevant business connection. This page does not transmit or store a request.

What happens next

After authority is verified, application-held operational information associated with that connection will be deleted or irreversibly anonymized within 30 days, except records that must be retained for security, fraud prevention, or legal obligations.

Deletion status

The authorized business administrator provides status through the same verified business channel used for the request. This public page does not expose private deletion records.

If you cannot sign in

Signing in is not required to revoke the application in Facebook Business Integrations. Contact the authorized business administrator through the verified channel that originally provided access.
