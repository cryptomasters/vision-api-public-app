Privacy policy

This policy describes the use of information by Ne Cong Alwel Tisinyuk, the private integration operated by authorized administrators of Alwel Tisinyuk.

Scope and information processed

The integration processes business and technical-user identifiers, authorized advertising-account, campaign, Page, catalog, product and measurement-resource metadata; access and permission information; and operational records needed to perform and verify administrator-requested work. Where a measurement connection is configured, it may process conversion-event and attribution information supplied through that authorized connection.

Access tokens and application credentials are used to authenticate the integration. They are kept in protected operational storage and are not included in this public site.

Purpose and limits

Information is used to perform authorized advertising and catalog operations, manage approved Page and measurement connections, report results, maintain security and investigate operational errors. Access is limited to the accounts, assets and permissions granted to this application. Information is not sold or used for unrelated profiling.

Sharing

Information is exchanged with Meta and the service providers required for the specific authorized operation, including configured tracking and reporting services. Access to operational records is restricted to authorized administrators. This public website does not disclose their inventory, identifiers or credentials.

Public website

This site has no analytics, advertising trackers, credential forms or application cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses and technical access logs, under its own privacy policy.

Retention and security

Operational information is retained while needed to provide the authorized service, investigate errors, protect the service or meet applicable legal obligations. Credentials are stored in operating-system-protected or restricted server storage. Access can be revoked in the applicable Meta authorization settings.

Requests and contact

To ask about access, correction or deletion, contact the authorized administrator through the verified business channel used when access was granted. Identify this application and describe the request without sending passwords, access tokens or application secrets.
