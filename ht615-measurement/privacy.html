Privacy Policy

This policy applies to HT615 Measurement Hub, a private owner-controlled integration used by authorized administrators for approved Meta measurement operations.

Effective date: August 18, 2026

Scope

The application is not a public consumer service. It is used only for the approved Business and technical actors configured by the owner.

Information processed

The application may process Meta business and technical-actor identifiers, measurement-resource identifiers and metadata, permission and relationship status, token validity metadata, and operational diagnostics required to perform an administrator-requested operation. Credentials are used only to authenticate authorized API requests and are not exposed through this site.

How information is used

Information is used only to prepare, validate and perform owner-approved measurement-resource operations, verify access, preserve audit evidence, and support security. The application does not sell personal data and does not use information for unrelated profiling.

Sharing

Information is shared only with Meta APIs, service providers strictly necessary to perform the requested operation, and authorized business administrators.

Retention and security

Application credentials are kept in operating-system-protected credential storage. Operational records are retained only as long as required for service delivery, security, audit or legal obligations. Portable evidence excludes raw secrets.

Your choices

Access can be revoked through Facebook Settings under Business Integrations or the applicable Meta authorization settings. Revocation stops future API access. Application-held information may be deleted as described on the Data Deletion page.

View Data Deletion instructions →

Contact

Privacy and deletion questions should be sent to the authorized business administrator through the same verified channel used to grant access. Do not send passwords, access tokens or application secrets.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the application's processing changes. The effective date identifies the current version.
